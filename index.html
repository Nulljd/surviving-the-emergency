Our Approach

We get your team ready to handle the emergency until fire and EMS arrive and take over — and we prepare you for a clean handoff, so the person who had the emergency gets the best possible outcome.

And we help you meet the requirements that come with it — your own compliance obligations and any third-party or client mandates — so your training checks the box and holds up when it's reviewed.

Emergency Action Planning

Site-specific emergency action plans covering evacuation, severe weather, medical events, and a clean hand-off to arriving EMS — the written backbone that ties your training and drills together.

Best for: Offices, schools, healthcare, warehouses, manufacturing, and public facilities.

CPR, AED & First Aid

Help employees respond quickly and confidently during cardiac, breathing, bleeding, and injury emergencies.

Best for: Offices, schools, healthcare, warehouses, manufacturing, and public facilities.

First Aid Training

Prepare employees to respond quickly to common workplace injuries, sudden illness, bleeding, burns, and other medical emergencies.

Best for: Offices, schools, healthcare teams, construction crews, warehouses, manufacturing, and public-facing workplaces.

Stop the Bleed

Bleeding-control training — direct pressure, wound packing, and tourniquet use — to stop life-threatening bleeding before EMS arrives.

Best for: Schools, public facilities, manufacturing, warehouses, and offices.

Bloodborne Pathogens

Help employees understand exposure risks, safe response procedures, and workplace infection control practices.

Best for: Healthcare, schools, public safety, janitorial teams, and workplace responders.
